# Airline Rebooking Policies

Retrievable chunks. Each chunk has a stable ID that recovery rationales cite
in square brackets (e.g. `[airline_rebooking:SQ-CANCEL-01]`).

## airline_rebooking:SQ-CANCEL-01
Singapore Airlines: when a scheduled flight is cancelled, ticketed passengers
are eligible for rebooking on the next available Singapore Airlines flight
to the same destination at no additional cost. If no SQ flight is available
within 24 hours, endorsement onto a partner (Star Alliance) carrier is
permitted.

## airline_rebooking:SQ-DELAY-01
Singapore Airlines: delays over 4 hours qualify the traveller for
complimentary meal service and, on international sectors, rebooking onto an
earlier or later same-day flight if that reduces overall delay.

## airline_rebooking:BA-CANCEL-01
British Airways: cancellation entitles rebooking within +/- 72 hours of the
original departure at no charge. Route changes to a nearby airport (e.g. LHR
→ LGW) are permitted when arriving within the same 24-hour window.

## airline_rebooking:BA-DELAY-01
British Airways: delays over 3 hours on flights over 3,500 km qualify for EU
compensation regime (EC 261) at £520 per passenger if the delay is within the
carrier's control.

## airline_rebooking:AF-CANCEL-01
Air France: cancellation on a Paris-hub flight entitles rebooking on the next
available AF or KLM flight within 24 hours at no cost. Rebooking on a
non-SkyTeam carrier requires supervisor approval.

## airline_rebooking:GENERIC-01
For carriers not otherwise listed, apply the traveller's ticket fare rules.
Basic economy fares typically forfeit the ticket on cancellation; standard
economy and above are rebookable within 24 hours.
